    Prediction methodology and limitations

    Dataset: data/crowd_history_sample.csv contains synthetic monthly indices for 2023–2025. These are demonstration values, not measured visitors or actual historic attendance.

    Method: for the selected calendar month, the projection starts with the latest sample-year index and adds the average annual change between the earliest and latest sample years. The month-by-month pattern supplies a simple seasonal baseline; the result is clamped to 0–100.

    Reading the result: Low is 0–35, Moderate is 36–65, and High is 66–100. Suggested periods are the three lowest monthly projections for the selected destination. Alternatives are catalogue destinations with a lower sample projection for the selected month.

    Limitations: there is no verified visitor-count history, live occupancy feed, event calendar, weather input, or access-condition feed behind this prototype. The synthetic pattern does not establish real seasonality, crowd levels, or the best time to visit. Treat all comparisons as UI/algorithm examples only and confirm conditions with current local or official sources.
